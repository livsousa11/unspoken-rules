Everybody knows them. Nobody taught you.

Some social rules are never clearly explained. This website collects those unwritten rules and explains what they mean, when they apply, and why people follow them.

Example Unspoken Rule

Don't swipe through someone else's photos.

Why? Their other photos may be private.

When? When someone hands you their phone to show you one specific picture.

What should I do instead? Hand the phone back or ask if you can see another picture.
